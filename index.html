THE ENTIRE STRATEGYBeat the dealer if you can.
Otherwise, lose with dignity.
1

Look at both dealer hands

That is the “face up” part. It would be rude not to use the information.

2

Find the best legal low hand

Try different two-card combinations to win both comparisons. The remaining five cards form your high hand, which must outrank the low hand. Some hands can take a minute.

3

Take the best available result

If you cannot win, look for a push. If every legal split loses, any legal set—or mucking—is optimal.

Joker note: the Joker is a semi-wild bug. It acts as an Ace, or completes a straight, flush, straight flush, or royal flush. A-2-3-4-5 is the second-highest straight.
